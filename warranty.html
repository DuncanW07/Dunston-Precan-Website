We build the DP-41 to last, and we stand behind it. Real support, from the people who designed it.

What is covered

For [2 years, to be confirmed] from delivery, we cover defects in materials and workmanship, including the Miyota 82S5 automatic movement, the case, crystal and dial. We will repair the watch or, if repair isn’t possible, replace it.

What isn’t covered

  • Normal wear: scratches, scuffs and the natural aging of leather straps and coatings.
  • Damage from accidents, impacts, misuse, or repairs and openings by anyone other than us or our authorized service partner.
  • Moisture damage beyond the watch’s confirmed water resistance rating, which will be listed on the product page once our manufacturer certifies it.
  • Normal timekeeping variation of a mechanical movement, within the movement maker’s published tolerance.

How to make a claim

Email [contact email to be confirmed] with your order number, a short description and photos if you can. We will reply with next steps and cover insured shipping both ways for valid claims.

Your rights

This warranty is in addition to any rights you have under consumer law.
