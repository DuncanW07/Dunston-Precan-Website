Privacy policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

We keep this short because we collect very little. Dunston Precan is a brand of Dunston Co. ("we", "us"). This policy covers this website.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you join our waitlist. We use it to tell you about our launch and nothing else.
  • Order details, once orders open: your name, shipping address, email and what you bought, so we can deliver your watch and support it.
  • Payment details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout page and handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

What we don't do

  • We never sell your information or share it with anyone for their own marketing.
  • We don't use tracking pixels, analytics or advertising cookies. If that ever changes, we'll ask first with a cookie banner and update this page.

Your cart

Your cart is saved in your browser's local storage, on your own device, so it's still there when you come back. It isn't sent to us until you check out. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Who helps us run the site

A few service providers process information on our behalf and only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase stores our waitlist and order records.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that signups come from people, not bots. It looks at technical signals from your browser and does not use cookies for advertising.
  • Stripe processes payments once orders open.

How we protect it

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database uses row-level security: visitors can add their email to the waitlist but can never read it, and customers can only see their own orders. Only we can read the waitlist.

How long we keep it

Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove them. Order records are kept as long as we need them for warranty, support, and tax and accounting obligations.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time, and every email we send has an unsubscribe link. To be removed from the waitlist or to make any request, email us at [contact email to be confirmed] and we'll take care of it promptly.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email, we'll tell you before it takes effect.
