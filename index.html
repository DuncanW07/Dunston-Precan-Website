Inside the DP-41

  • Miyota 82S5 automatic. Japanese, self-winding, open heart, no battery.
  • 41 mm 316L steel case. 47.6 mm lug to lug, 21 mm lugs, 11.52 mm thick.
  • Sapphire crystal. Ø33.00 × 1.50 mm, anti-reflective inside, plus a sapphire display caseback.
  • Fluted bezel. 72 sharp V-flutes.
  • Côtes de Genève dial. Vertical stripes, floating index ring, 12 applied markers with lume.
  • Signature details. Ring-hub minute hand, lance hour hand, numbered seconds disc, AUTOMATIC key-bridge.
